## What changed

### The Resource Committee picks the model (P72 T0b)

A new opt-in mod, `resource-committee` (`/plugin install resource-committee@xmarks`),
rewrites the model and effort of each main-thread request through the
`turn.step` hook. Every session starts at `sonnet/medium`. Each idle prompt is
classified once by Haiku as mechanical (`sonnet/low`), routine (`sonnet/medium`)
or judgment (`opus/high`), and the whole turn runs at that route. It never
switches mid-turn, because caches are model-scoped. `/committee` shows the
decision or pins a tier. Subagents keep the model they were given. Model ids
are first-party; on Bedrock or Vertex, pin a tier or leave the mod uninstalled.

Until now a session could not switch its own model, so plans asking for
`sonnet/medium` ran on whatever model the session had started on, which
check-route-honored recorded as "session model; not switched".

### `/flywheel:route`'s model table

`skills/route/references/models.md` now names Claude Sonnet 5.5 (same price as
Sonnet 5, effort levels recalibrated), takes the 2026-09-25 snapshot, and says
why routing belongs at turn boundaries.
