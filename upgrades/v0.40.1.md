## What changed

- **`skills/work/evals/check.sh`**: the first assertion of both katas required
  the **first** `.check-log` entry to be `RESULT=FAIL` at the pristine
  `IMPL_SHA`. It now requires a `RESULT=FAIL` at the pristine `IMPL_SHA` with
  **no entry of a changed `IMPL_SHA` before it** — which is the property the
  expectation always stated in prose ("the test ran red before `cart.py`
  changed"), rather than one particular way of arriving at it.
- **`scripts/test-eval-graders.sh`**: two new cases, written red first — a log
  with a green baseline run before the red must pass, and a log that never went
  red must fail. The existing test-after and red-on-untouched guards are
  unchanged and still hold.
- **`skills/work/evals/evals.json` + `evals/README.md`**: expectation wording
  brought in line with what the grader now mechanizes.

## Why

The v0.40.0 release gate ran the `work` katas and eval 2 came back 5/6, three
times running. The failure was not the skill: every trace showed the test going
red against an untouched `cart.py`. What the executors did was run
`./run-tests.sh` once to confirm a green baseline **before** writing the test —
reasonable, and what `/flywheel:debug` prescribes — which puts a `PASS` at the
pristine sha in front of the red and broke the "first entry" mechanization.

The decisive run: the same kata, executed against the **pre-P28** skill text
from `origin/main`, failed identically. That is what established the red as
pre-existing grader drift rather than a regression, and it is why the fix ships
as its own release instead of inside the one it was gating — loosening an
assertion in the release that assertion is gating is exactly what P26 exists to
prevent.

Evidence: `skills/work/evals/benchmarks/2026-09-09/`. All four real gate
workdirs re-grade green under the fixed grader; the synthetic guards stay red
where they should.
