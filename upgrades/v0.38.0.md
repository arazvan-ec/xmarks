## What changed

- **`skills/spec/SKILL.md`**, **`skills/process/SKILL.md`**, **README**: the
  P21 "materialize the permission" examples switch from `Bash(npm test*)` /
  `Bash(psql*)` to `Bash(npm test:*)` / `Bash(psql:*)`, and spec/process now
  say to always emit the `:*` suffix form. `:*` is documented as equivalent to
  the trailing ` *` (bare command included) without the bare-`*` trap of also
  matching unrelated longer commands.
- Rules already written into repos by earlier sign-offs still match; replacing
  a `Bash(cmd*)` with `Bash(cmd:*)` in `.claude/settings.json` is a safe,
  optional one-line tightening.
