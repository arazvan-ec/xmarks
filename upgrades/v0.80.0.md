## What changed

Leftovers from an independent review of 0.79.0 (no blocking findings):

- **`+delegate` needs a check.** It hands a tier-1 subagent to the `executor`
  agent, which answers `DONE: <check>` or `ESCALATE`. Read-only jobs such as
  "summarise this log" have no check to give it, so they now go to a plain
  subagent with `model` instead.
- **Step 2.3 reads in order again.** The rename sentence had taken the arrow
  meant for the subagent route.
- The 0.79.0 note now states its renumber history correctly, and the route
  spec's Verify section records the second S2 run.
