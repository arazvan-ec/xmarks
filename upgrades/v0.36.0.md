## What changed

- **`skills/verify/evals/fixtures/tally-{fail,sneaky,pass}/README.md`** now
  describe the app and point at the spec, nothing more. Previously they carried
  the answer key into the workdir: `tally-sneaky`'s said the defect "lives only in
  the CLI path", warned that "a verifier that only runs the tests and
  rationalizes from there will wrongly PASS", and gave the required verdict
  verbatim; the other two stated the correct verdict outright.
- **Ground truth moved to `skills/verify/evals/README.md`** — a file that is never
  copied and that executors are told not to read — as a table of planted defect
  and only-correct-verdict per fixture, with the rule spelled out: *nothing
  describing the assertions, the planted defect, or the expected verdict may live
  inside a fixture, and a fixture may not announce that it is one.*
- **`skills/verify/evals/benchmarks/2026-07-29/COMPROMISED.md`** states what the
  leak does and does not invalidate. The **+30 pp headline (10/10 vs 7/10) holds**:
  the delta is the machine-parseable verdict line, the leak stated that exact
  format, and the baseline still closed in prose all three times — so the hint
  worked against the result rather than for it. The bug-detection,
  evidence-citation and ran-the-real-CLI assertions do **not** hold; both arms
  passed them and the benchmark had already flagged them as non-discriminating.
- **The fixture's `specs/csv-tally.md` deliberately stays** in the workdir. Stating
  the success metric is the contract the verifier checks against, not a leak.
- **Spec bookkeeping**: the P23 and P25 spec `Status` blocks said the release was
  "held at the bump" and `upgrades/` untouched, both false once those gates ran
  and shipped. Corrected, and P25 now records the measured outcome of its
  requirement 1 (the kata did not discriminate).
- **A third leak, found by the mechanical check itself**: the `work` fixtures'
  `run-tests.sh` carried a comment saying the audit line makes "whether the impl
  was still pristine at red time mechanically checkable" — and an executor has to
  read that file to use it. Rewritten to describe what the script does without
  saying what is graded; verified the runner still writes `.check-log` and still
  refuses a direct `unittest`. Both eval READMEs now carry the grep as a
  mandatory pre-iteration step.
- **Two more, found by re-running the check after each fix** — the pillar-2
  fixtures (`demo-repo`, `target-repo`) opened with "Eval fixture for flywheel
  pillar-2 skills", and the `work` guard message I added in v0.34.0 said the
  runner "records the audit log this kata is **graded** from". Both told the
  executor it was being evaluated. Rewritten neutrally; verified the guard still
  refuses a direct `unittest`, the runner still writes `.check-log`, and all three
  pillar-2 graders are still red on an untouched fixture. Pillar-2's leak mattered
  less so far because those gates ran with-skill only, but it would have poisoned
  the baseline arm that is still on the follow-up list.
- **Backlog: P26** — `verify` and `work` have no committed `check.sh`, so nobody
  can run "can this assertion even fail?" the way pillar 2's grader allowed. Both
  pillar-1 defects found this week were found by accident.

## Open, and stated rather than implied

No iteration has run against the clean `verify` fixtures, so the bug-detection
assertions currently have **no trustworthy measurement**. Re-running is the
follow-up.
