## What changed

- **`/flywheel:spec`** (`skills/spec/SKILL.md`) — priming now starts from the
  repo's product definition when one exists (`PRODUCT.md`, or the north-star
  in `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`): a spec's Reasons must anchor in what the repo
  is building, and a feature the product definition doesn't cover starts by
  revising that definition in the same spec.
- **`/flywheel:process`** (`skills/process/SKILL.md`) — same priming step; a
  contract's Purpose must name the business capability it implements.

## Why

Owner direction (2026-07-27): a repo where flywheel is installed carries its
own reason to exist — its features and business live in the repo, and any AI
runtime (Claude Code, Manus, Codex, …) must pick that context up before
building. The plugin ships the mechanism (read-and-anchor); each repo owns
its product definition. First user: the `keep` repo's `PRODUCT.md` (a
personal tracking & development app evolved from the owner's content).
