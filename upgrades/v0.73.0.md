## What changed

`/flywheel:plan` routes every plan task to a model and effort tier (P27), but
its spec left everything outside a plan out of scope. A child session, a loose
subagent or a `/flywheel:run` execution still inherited the caller's model, and
a subagent inherited the caller's framing along with it.

`/flywheel:route <task>` closes that gap with a fixed decision order. The first
question that answers wins:

1. A deterministic tool already does it → `tool`, no model.
2. The work carries a point of view (research, grading, adversarial review,
   synthesis) → `fresh-session`. Never a subagent, and never a `fork`, which
   inherits the whole context and ignores `model`.
3. Many identical reads with no judgment → `subagent` at tier 1, in parallel.
4. Anything else → `here`, at the tier its difficulty calls for.

It lowers effort before it lowers the model, and judges cost per completed
task. Tiers are read from `scripts/route-tiers.txt`, which stays the single
authority. Dated prices live in `skills/route/references/models.md` and load
only when two routes are close. It recommends; the caller decides.
