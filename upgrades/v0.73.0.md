## What changed

`route_escalated_from` said **that** a task left the plan's route; nothing said
**why**. The tree held 19 escalations, 0 reasons, and 18 of the 19 were the same
move, `sonnet/medium → opus/high`. The ladder was being declined by habit, and
the record could not tell a task that needed more from one too small to be worth
a subagent, or from nobody trying.

- **`route_reason` is required** on a line that carries `route_escalated_from`
  or drops a planned `+delegate`. From the new `route-reason` cutoff
  (`scripts/cutoffs.txt`, `2026-09-23T18:00:00Z`) a deviation without a
  non-empty reason fails `scripts/check-route-honored.sh`. The 19 earlier ones
  are counted, not backfilled (P18). `FLYWHEEL_ROUTE_REASON_FROM` moves the cut.
- A line with `route_escalated_from` and **no `route` field** still needs its
  reason (Codex, PR #95): the escalation is checked independently of `route`.
- **The reasons are listed in one place**: the gate prints each explained
  deviation as `route-honored: deviation — <slug> <task> <planned> → <ran>: <reason>`.
- `/flywheel:work` and its `work-detail.md` field list say to record it.

A cycle already running when this lands, and writing escalation lines after the
cutoff, needs `route_reason` on them before its gate is green.
