## What changed

`/flywheel:plan` routes every plan task to a model and effort tier (P27), but
its spec left everything outside a plan out of scope. A child session, a loose
subagent or a `/flywheel:run` execution still inherited the caller's model, and
a subagent inherited the caller's framing along with it.

`/flywheel:route <task>` closes that gap: a fixed decision order picks the
mechanism (tool, fresh session, subagent or here) and then the tier. The order
and its exceptions live in `skills/route/SKILL.md` and nowhere else. The one
rule worth knowing before reading it: work that carries a point of view goes
to a fresh session, never to a subagent or a `fork`.

It lowers effort before it lowers the model, and judges cost per completed
task. Tiers are read from `scripts/route-tiers.txt`, which stays the single
authority. Dated prices, context sizes and effort support live in
`skills/route/references/models.md`, loaded only by the steps that need them.
It recommends; the caller decides.
