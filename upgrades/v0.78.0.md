## What changed

About eleven version collisions were renumbered by hand, and the one in #81
shipped three stale pointers, one inside an `::error::` remedy (P59).

- **`scripts/renumber.sh <old> <new> [base]`** does the mechanics: `git mv`
  of `upgrades/v<old>.md` and every `*-v<old>` dir, then the note's frontmatter
  `version:` and `plugin.json`. It refuses a `<new>` that is not ahead of the
  base, or whose note already exists.
- **`renumber.sh --check <old> [base]`** lists every `<old>` token (x.y.z, or a
  P-number like `P55`) left in files the branch touches, as `file:line`, and
  exits 1 while any is listed. `.claude/flywheel/runs/**` and lines carrying
  `Renumbered from` are exempt. History is kept by adding
  `<!-- renumber: keep -->` on the line; nothing else classifies a leftover.
- **`skills/ship/references/renumber.md`** is the template for the judgment:
  how to tell a pointer from history, and the P-number variant. `/flywheel:ship`
  gains a merge-the-base step that cites it and pre-approves the script.

It cannot see files the branch did not touch; a stale pointer there is still
`check-version-citations.sh`'s job. 0.77.0 belongs to P60.
