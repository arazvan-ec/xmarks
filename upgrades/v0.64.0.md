## What changed

Asked the plain question the cost convention implies — **which phase of the loop
costs most?** — the ledger could not answer it, for two reasons measured on
flywheel's own corpus (11 cycles, 58 transitions):

- **`phase` was optional.** `check-telemetry.sh` accepted `task` *or* `phase`,
  and 36 of 58 lines carry none; 6 of 11 cycles carry none on any line. Those
  lines identify their transition perfectly well — they just cannot be summed
  with the rest. Identification and aggregation are different duties, and only
  the first was ever specified.
- **There was no view across runs.** `run-cost.sh` took one run and one
  baseline, so the cross-cycle roll-up had to be hand-written to ask at all.

Two changes:

1. **A line written from the cutoff on names its `phase`** — the gate fails
   otherwise. The cutoff (`2026-09-17T20:00:00Z`, overridable with
   `FLYWHEEL_PHASE_REQUIRED_FROM`) sits after the newest line in the corpus and
   before the first line of the cycle that shipped this, so the rule binds live
   work from the moment it exists rather than lying dormant. Lines older than
   the cutoff stay a **counted notice**: nothing is backfilled (P18), and a
   date-granular cutoff would have reddened two of that same day's lines on
   history that cannot be fixed.
2. **`run-cost.sh --all <runs-dir>`** merges every run file and reports the
   corpus by phase, by route and by cycle. The per-FIELD coverage discipline
   from P40a is carried through the merge, so a phase whose lines predate a
   field reports it UNMEASURED instead of totalling the absence as zero, and
   phase-less lines are bucketed apart rather than attributed to a phase.

`skills/work` states the duty in one clause in the body; the shape lives in
`skills/work/references/work-detail.md`.

Nothing to do on upgrade: existing telemetry stays valid and is reported as the
debt it is. Runs from here on carry `phase`.
