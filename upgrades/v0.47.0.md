## What changed

- **`scripts/check-invocation-budget.sh` (P36)**: enforces **two** numbers per
  skill instead of one — `body` (the file, as before) and `worst` (body + every
  distinct `references/*.md` reachable from it, followed transitively with cycle
  protection, each file charged once however often it is cited). `worst` is an
  upper bound on purpose: moving a hot-path rule out of the body no longer
  improves the measured number, which is the move the old measure rewarded.
  Dangling citations are now chased one level down, and an exception naming no
  skill fails as stale.

- **`scripts/invocation-budget.txt` (P36)**: keyed format — `body` / `worst`
  defaults plus `<skill> body=<n> worst=<n>` exception lines, each carrying the
  reason it exists. A bare number from the old format exits 2 with the migration
  spelled out rather than being coerced into one of the two ceilings.

- **`scripts/delegation-guard.sh` (P37)**: the TIER family resolves a
  subagent's `subagent_type` to its agent definition (`.claude/agents/` first,
  then the plugin's `agents/`) and treats a pinned `model:` / `effort:` as the
  decision it is. `create_session` is unchanged — it really does inherit — and
  CONTEXT and FANOUT still apply in full.

- **`scripts/check-hook-parity.sh` (P38, new)** + CI wiring: runs the installer
  into a throwaway target, reads the settings it produced, and diffs the
  `(event, matcher, script)` triples against `hooks/hooks.json` in both
  directions, plus a landed-and-executable check on `bin/`.

## Why these three

Measured, not asserted. P35 moved 13,713 B out of six bodies and every one of
the six grew in **total**, because the reference a step cites is read by the
same invocation that was credited for not having it inline:

| skill | body before P35 | body now | refs | worst |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `work` | 8,385 | 5,259 | 5,986 | **11,245** |
| `process` | 8,280 | 4,458 | 5,873 | 10,331 |
| `help` | 8,395 | 4,342 | 4,537 | 8,879 |

`skills/work/SKILL.md` cites its one reference from three steps a normal cycle
reaches, so the gate printed `OK — worst case work at 5,259/5,300` while the
real cost had risen a third. P35's own closing line asked for this revision:
*"Revisit with per-skill exceptions, or by splitting `work`."*

The guard and the parity gate are the same class of defect one layer down: an
instrument that is not measuring what it claims. A guard that fires on its
project's own hot path gets clicked through, and two hand-maintained copies of
one hook list disagree the moment someone edits one of them — which is exactly
what 0.44.0 shipped.

## No action required

No behavior changes in any skill, agent or prompt: this release touches gates,
one hook's asking threshold, and CI. `requires-action: false`.

Two debts are recorded rather than hidden. `work` (11,245 B worst case) and
`process` (10,331) hold named ceiling exceptions; the honest follow-up for
`work` is splitting the skill, not a larger number. The parity gate proves the
two wirings **agree**, never that either is correct.
